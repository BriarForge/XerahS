## CORE-025 watch publication and retention candidate — 2026-11-14

Maintainer-approved CORE-PLATFORM-001 0.6.0 under contract 0.4.0 resolves the watch publication and retention policy on Linux. The watch checkpoint store is version 2 with `items` and `publications`; a version 1 store is read and upgraded on the next write, and the watch rules store stays version 1. The publication disposition and the retention rule are pure production helpers that the service and the conformance adapter both call, so the policy is not implemented twice.

The send intent is durably saved before any dispatch. When the intent cannot be saved, no HTTP request is sent, watching stops, and the failure is reported as `watch-store-write-failed`. The acknowledgement test is proof over every attempt: the upload executor counts the attempts that ended with an HTTP status and reports that count beside the attempt count, so the last status alone cannot decide the publication. A publication left over as `sending` from an earlier process is unconfirmed, is never uploaded again automatically under its key, and is named in the startup notice without any path or URL. Local copy and move are unchanged.

Evidence: the disposition order, the retention rule, the version 1 read and next-write upgrade, the version 2 refusal and the durable intent are covered by `WatchFoldersTest`. `WatchFolderServiceTest` covers a request stopped mid-flight, a request stopped before it started, an intent that cannot be saved, and a leftover sending record. `WatchKillProofTest` is the real process SIGKILL proof on a private tmpfs: a separate process uploads against a local server that never answers, the parent kills it with SIGKILL while the request is in flight, and the restarted process sends no second request and reports the publication as unconfirmed. `UploadExecutorTest` counts the answered attempts, so a timeout followed by a server error and an exhausted retry is not fully answered. All nineteen CORE-025 contract vectors pass through the shared production helpers on Qt 6.12 and on the approved Qt 6.4.2 baseline.

The mutations that must fail do fail: removing the durable send intent fails the SIGKILL proof, turning an unknown publication into a released one fails the disposition order and the stop-mid-request journey, removing the missing-parent guard fails the retention tests, and deriving the acknowledgement from the last HTTP status fails the stop-mid-request journey.

The exact remaining gap is outside CORE-025 and is not claimed here: network and FUSE qualification is a separate Layer 5 concern under CORE-025 P7, and the periodic scan fallback already exists. The independent model review of the upload executor change required by ROOT-SECURITY-001, the maintainer line review and the full parent gates are review gates on this work, not missing behavior. Windows and macOS remain unimplemented for CORE-025 and keep their own dispositions; no platform is waived, and no activation, census closure or release approval is claimed. Coverage moves to 178/324.
